## What You Are Actually Paying For

A shed quote is not one flat number. It is built from four layers:

1. **Base structure:** size and core materials
2. **Customization:** doors, windows, layout, finish
3. **Site factors:** access, slope, prep, foundation
4. **Local requirements:** permit or HOA constraints when they apply

That is why two sheds that look identical online can have different final prices in two backyards.

## 7 Factors That Change Your Final Price

### 1) Size and footprint

Size is the obvious driver, but it is not just square footage. Bigger footprints can mean more prep work, tighter placement constraints, and a wider gap between a basic and an upgraded build. Start from what you will store or do in the shed, then consider the room you may need later.

#### 8x10 vs 10x12: compare the layout before the price

An 8x10 footprint is 80 square feet; a 10x12 is 120 square feet. The 10x12 adds 40 square feet, or 50% more footprint. Those are outside-dimension comparisons, not a promise of clear interior space.

| Planning question | 8x10 | 10x12 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Footprint | 80 sq. ft. | 120 sq. ft. |
| November 2026 starting-price example | $2,880 | $3,600 |
| First layout to sketch | Shelving and the largest item, with a path to the door | The same items, plus the extra space you intend to use |
| Check before deciding | Door opening, shelf depth, and room to retrieve items | Those same clearances, plus whether the larger footprint fits the property |

Mark each footprint in the yard and measure the items you plan to store. Sketch their depth, the door swing, and an access path. Keep wall height, roof, doors, and upgrades the same when comparing current estimates so the difference reflects the size choice. For a finished 10x12 example, inspect the [Boerne storage project's photos and build details](/projects/boerne/10x12x8-storage-shed-boerne); its 8-foot walls and selected finishes describe that project, not every base package.

### 2) Frame, siding, roof, and floor materials

Compare the materials in each part of the building. Metal siding does not describe the frame or floor. Our [10x12x8 Boerne storage shed](/projects/boerne/10x12x8-storage-shed-boerne), for example, has an open wood-framed interior and upgraded vertical metal panels.

That example describes one completed project. Before approving your own quote, confirm the frame, siding, roof, floor, ventilation, and applicable warranty coverage. Share how you will use the shed and any HOA finish requirements so the proposed building can be checked against your needs.

### 3) Doors, windows, and layout

This is where first-time buyers most often overspend. A wider door, one well-placed window, or a smarter layout can genuinely improve daily use. A pile of add-ons chosen from photos usually just inflates the price. Pick upgrades based on how you will use the shed every week.

### 4) Site access and yard conditions

A flat backyard with a wide gate is the easy case. Narrow side yards, tight gates, slopes, and obstacles change the work. This is where building on site pays off: because we construct your shed in your yard instead of hauling in a finished building on a trailer, a gate that would stop a prefab delivery truck usually is not a problem for us. Send clear yard and gate photos early so your quote reflects reality instead of assumptions.

For a Hill Country property, our [Boerne shed planning page](/service-area/boerne) outlines access and site questions to discuss and shows completed local examples.

### 5) Foundation and San Antonio soil

Much of the San Antonio area sits on expansive clay that swells when wet and shrinks in drought. That movement is exactly why the foundation approach matters more here than in a lot of the country. The right choice depends on your soil, drainage, shed size, and use. Ask why a specific foundation is being recommended for your yard, not in general.

### 6) Permits and HOA rules

The [City of San Antonio's no-permit-required list](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/DSD/Constructing/Residential/Permits/No-Permit-Required) says one-story detached tool and storage sheds of 300 square feet or less typically do not require a building permit when setback requirements are met. The same page notes that historic, neighborhood conservation, and corridor districts can have design and material restrictions.

Confirm the review path for your address, intended use, foundation, utilities, and design with the applicable authority. Do not apply a San Antonio rule to a Boerne, Helotes, or other jurisdiction. HOA review is a separate check. Our [permits and HOA guide](/news/sanantonio_shed_guide) links to planning resources; confirm current requirements and any fees before finalizing your scope.

### 7) Timeline and complexity

Deeper customization and complicated sites add labor. Clear scope decisions upfront prevent expensive rework later. Our typical build takes one day on site, which keeps labor predictable and means your backyard is not a construction zone for weeks.

## Hidden Costs First-Time Buyers Miss

Most budget stress comes from line items nobody discussed early:

- **Site prep:** leveling, clearing, or access adjustments
- **Old shed removal:** if you are replacing an existing structure, ask for this as a line item upfront
- **Electrical planning:** even if you are not wiring it now, placement decisions today affect that cost later
- **HOA revision loops:** repeated design changes to satisfy an HOA can stretch your timeline
- **Vague quotes:** a low number with no detail usually means scope gaps that surface after you commit

A good quote is not just a final number. It shows what is included, what is optional, and what assumptions were made about your site.

## Custom vs Prefab vs DIY

| Path | Usually best for | Common cost risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Custom build | Fit-for-purpose design with fewer compromises | Over-customizing without a clear use plan |
| Prefab | Fast and minimal decisions | Paying later to fix fit, access, or quality tradeoffs |
| DIY | Time, tools, and confidence to manage it all | Underestimating time, complexity, and rework |

All three can work. But note that a prefab price usually does not include site prep, foundation, or solving your access problem, and a DIY budget rarely survives contact with reality. ## A 5-Minute Budgeting Worksheet

Before requesting quotes, write down:

1. **Primary use:** storage, workshop, office, or mixed
2. **Must-haves:** essentials only
3. **Nice-to-haves:** kept separate from essentials
4. **Site realities:** access path, slope, drainage, obstacles
5. **Approval constraints:** HOA standards and city rules
6. **Timeline:** when you want it done

Then tag every feature as **need now**, **can add later**, or **skip**. This one step keeps your quote aligned with your real priorities instead of impulse upgrades.

### Do I need a permit for a shed in San Antonio?

San Antonio's published exemption generally covers one-story detached tool and storage sheds no larger than 300 square feet that meet setback requirements. Other uses, district restrictions, utilities, and HOA rules need separate checks. Confirm the requirements for your address and design using the [permits and HOA guide](/news/sanantonio_shed_guide) and the applicable authority.

### Why do two sheds the same size have different prices?

Dimensions are only the baseline. Wall height, roof style, doors, windows, upgrades, and site work can change the scope. Compare configurations with the same choices, then check which items are included in the build subtotal and which still need separate pricing.

### Is the cheapest shed the best value?

Compare the complete scope before deciding. Ask whether each quote includes the same dimensions, materials, floor, doors, build labor, site work, and warranty coverage. A lower subtotal may describe a different building or leave work to be priced later.

### What materials should I compare in a shed quote?

Ask for the frame, siding, roof, and floor materials separately. Then compare the proposed ventilation, maintenance requirements, warranty coverage, and any HOA restrictions. A completed-project photo can help you understand a particular design, but confirm the materials included in your own quote.

### Can I get an accurate price without a site visit?

The instant quote provides an estimated build subtotal for your choices. Clear yard and access photos help with the next review, but the final price still depends on confirmed travel, site conditions, and scope. Ask whether a site visit is needed for your property.
